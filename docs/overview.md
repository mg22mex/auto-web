# Product overview

## Mission

Autosell.MX helps buyers in Chihuahua discover, compare, finance, and inquire about guaranteed seminuevos — with a fast, mobile-first experience that routes high-intent leads to the sales team via WhatsApp and showroom appointments.

## Primary audiences

1. **Buyers** — browsing SUV / pickup / sedan inventory, simulating monthly payments, contacting sales.
2. **Sellers / trade-ins** — evaluating “Vende tu auto” and appointment flows.
3. **Sales advisors** — receiving pre-filled WhatsApp messages with vehicle + buyer context.
4. **Operators** — maintaining catalog filters, brand consistency, and lead hygiene.

## Product pillars

1. **Discover** — hero search, featured cars, filterable catalog.
2. **Decide** — vehicle detail, specs, estimated mensualidad, guarantees.
3. **Finance** — interactive payment calculator (enganche, plazo, tasa de referencia).
4. **Convert** — dual-path leads: form submit → WhatsApp, or one-tap WhatsApp CTAs.
5. **Trust** — GARANTIZADO badge, showroom info, JSON-LD for SEO.
